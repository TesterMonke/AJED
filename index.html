Linear Equations

Lesson overview

Solving equations one step at a time

An equation shows two expressions that are equal. To solve it, keep both sides balanced.

Variables

A variable is a letter that stands for an unknown number. In algebra, the most common variable is x.

Constants

A constant is a fixed number, like 5, 12, or 3.

Equations

An equation tells us two sides are equal, for example x + 5 = 12.

Equality

What you do to one side, you must do to the other side. This keeps the equation balanced.

One-step equations

A one-step equation can be solved with a single operation, such as adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing.

Two-step equations

A two-step equation needs two operations to isolate the variable, such as 2x + 3 = 15.

Worked example

x + 5 = 12

Subtract 5 from both sides:

x + 5 - 5 = 12 - 5 x = 7

Answer: x = 7
